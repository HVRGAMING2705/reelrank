What should you watch next?

Pick 1–3 movies you love. ReelRank builds your taste profile (the mean of their 216-feature vectors) and ranks all 64 movies by cosine similarity — genre one-hot (×2), keywords, era and runtime — no backend, no API calls.

How it works

1. Catalog
64 synthetic movies (1990–2026) with 12 genres, keyword tags, era bins (90s/2000s/2010s/2020s) and runtime bins (<100 / 100–140 / >140 min).
2. Features
Each movie becomes a 216-dim vector: genre one-hot (weight ×2), keyword one-hot, era one-hot, runtime one-hot — then L2-normalized.
3. Query
Your picks are averaged into one "taste profile" vector (also L2-normalized). Cosine similarity to every other movie gives the ranking — the exact same math train.py uses.
4. Evaluated
Mean genre-agreement precision@5 of 100.0% over all 64 movies (vs 27.8% for a random recommender — 3.6× lift).
